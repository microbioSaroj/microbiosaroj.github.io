Conducted a hospital-based study on the prevalence of Salmonella Typhi in febrile patients visiting the Kathmandu Model Hospital and evaluated resistance to fluoroquinolones associated with gyrA ser83 mutation in Salmonella Typhi and Paratyphi isolated from the patients. The research findings were published in Microbiology Insights.{% cite khadka2021antimicrobial %}
